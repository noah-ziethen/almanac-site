Almanac

A personal calendar app for the reMarkable 1 tablet.

Almanac shows Google Calendar on a reMarkable tablet in day, week, month and list views. Events can be added and deleted on the tablet, with titles typed or handwritten, and changes sync with Google Calendar over Wi‑Fi.

It is a personal project, run by its author on their own tablet. It is not offered as a public service, and it is not affiliated with Google or reMarkable.
